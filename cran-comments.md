## Submission

This is the first submission of ModalNet to CRAN.

The package implements the penalized (Lasso, elastic net, ridge, adaptive
Lasso) parametric modal regression of Galarza, Lachos and Liu for positive
responses, using 'glmnet' as the inner solver.
